## Leadership and Management Development:
From aspiring leaders to senior leaders, we offer ILM qualifications, as
well as micro training for areas such as managing teams, developing
motivation in teams, responding to disagreements in the workplace,
change leadership, culture, and inclusive leadership.
## Workplace Health and Wellbeing:
To help deal with many rising issues within workplaces, we offer training
on areas such as mental health awareness, supporting staff with ADHD
or Autism and Neurodiversity awareness. Other areas of support on offer
are stress management, resilience training for staff and compassionate
conversations.
## Digital, AI and Technology Skills:
To improve these skills, we offer short courses in digital communication
tools, digital information management, introduction to AI, AI for
workplaces as well as practical skills for using other technology in
workplaces.
## Compliance and Legislation:
Responding to the Employments Rights Act and other areas of
employment law, we offer short courses on areas such as employment
law for managers, sexual harassment training for managers, and equity,
diversity, and inclusive workplace training as well as data governance.
## Health and Safety:
We offer courses from first aid training, to fire safety, to workplace safety.
This includes practical training for safe and healthy working practices,
such as food safety and manual handling.
## Workplace Skills:
We are seeing a rise in need for training for soft skills in workplaces; we
can offer support through training in effective communication, customer
service skills, presentation and communication skills for workplaces,
collaboration skills, time management, problem solving and making
decisions using data. Influencing and negotiation skills courses are also
available to upskill staff.
## Welsh Language Skills:
Many employers are asking for provision of Welsh language for their staff,
so we can offer Welsh language learning, Welsh writing for workplaces
and confidence-building in using Welsh in the workplace.
## Land and Machinery:
Build the practical skills and recognised competence you need to work
safely and confidently in land-based industries. From tractors and
chainsaws to pesticides, our qualifications help you demonstrate that
you meet relevant industry and licence-to-practise standards.
